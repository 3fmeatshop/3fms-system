Users & PINs

Each person logs in with their own User ID + password (at the first login they use their PIN once, then set their own password). The PIN stays for the Time Clock and manager approvals. Forgot password? Tap 🔑 Reset password, save, and give them the new one-time password — they choose their own at the next login. Passwords are scrambled: you cannot see them. Their name is saved on every check, approval, dispatch, receiving, batch and sale.
