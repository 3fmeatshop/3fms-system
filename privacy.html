Privacy Notice

3F Meatshop · Santa Ana, Cagayan, Philippines · follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) · Updated November 12, 2026

What we collect

Your name, username, mobile number, email, delivery address and barangay; your orders, payments and photos of payment receipts; delivery photos taken by our rider. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares only your name and email address with us — we do not get your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.

Why we use it

To create your account, take and confirm your orders, prepare and deliver them, collect payment, send you login codes, answer your questions and keep the sales records the law requires. We do not sell your data or send you ads without asking.

Who can see it

Only 3F Meatshop staff who handle your order (sales, preparation, rider, manager). It is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database, Singapore), Google (records, emails and Google sign-in) and Cloudflare (website protection) — they only keep it for us.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. Sales and receipt records are kept as long as the tax law (BIR) requires, then deleted.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt) — nobody, not even the shop, can see your password. Access is limited to staff accounts, and the connection is encrypted (https).

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent (we then close your account, except records the law says we must keep). You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Contact

3F Meatshop, Santa Ana, Cagayan — call or message the shop, or message us on our Facebook page.
