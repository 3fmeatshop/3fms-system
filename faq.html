Frequently asked questions

3F Meatshop online shop · Santa Ana, Cagayan
How do I order?

Choose your products in the Shop, put them in your Cart, then tap Place order. You need a free customer account so the shop can deliver to your address.

When will my order arrive?

On the delivery date shown on your order — usually the day after the order is confirmed. There is no same-day delivery. The rider calls or texts you when the order is on the way.

Where does 3F Meatshop deliver?

Within the barangays of Santa Ana, Cagayan. Choose your barangay at checkout.

How much is the delivery fee?

It depends on your barangay and is shown at checkout. Bigger orders get free delivery — the shop shows how many kilos are needed.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, GCash / Maya, or bank transfer. For GCash, Maya or bank, type the reference number or attach the receipt; the order is confirmed once the payment is checked.

Why can the final amount be different from my order?

Meat is cut and weighed for each order, so the prepared weight can be a little more or less than what you ordered. You pay for the weight delivered, and it is shown on your order before delivery.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes — open it in My purchases and tap Cancel order any time until it is out for delivery. Please choose a reason. After that, call or message the shop.

Can I return items?

Check your items when the rider arrives. If something is wrong, damaged or not in good condition, refuse it at the door before paying — it is not charged. Once received and paid, items can no longer be returned, because meat is a perishable food. See the Terms & Refund Policy.

How do I track my order?

Every order has a tracking number (e.g. W00012-7K4Q-Z2HM) shown on your order. Tap Track order and type it — no need to log in. When logged in, all your orders are in My purchases.

Is there a voucher for new customers?

Yes — ₱50 off your first order of at least ₱500, for retail customers, valid 7 days from sign-up. It is applied automatically at checkout.

Do you sell wholesale or supply businesses?

Yes. Restaurants, resellers and companies can ask for a wholesale or bulk buyer account — message or call the shop.

How do I contact the shop?

Tap Help › Contact the shop to message on Messenger or call.

Is my personal data safe?

Your details are used only to take, deliver and follow up your orders, and are protected under the Data Privacy Act. See the Privacy Notice.

Still have a question? Tap Help › Contact the shop on the shop.
