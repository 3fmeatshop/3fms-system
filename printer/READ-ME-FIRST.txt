3F PRINTER HELPER - print receipts directly (no print window), cut the paper, open the cash drawer

INSTALL (once, on the POS computer only)
1. Copy this whole "printer" folder to the POS computer (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click  INSTALL-3F-Printer.bat
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click  More info  then  Run anyway.
   - When it says DONE, press any key.
3. Open the 3FMS POS in Chrome, press the  printer button  (Receipt printer settings):
   - Printing: Direct - no print window
   - Receipt printer: choose your thermal printer (e.g. POS-80 / XP-80)
   - Press  Test print .  Chrome asks once to let the site use apps on this device: click  Allow .
   - Press  Save .

From now on the receipt prints by itself after YES, the paper is cut and the
cash drawer opens on every cash sale. The helper starts by itself with Windows.

REMOVE: double-click REMOVE-3F-Printer.bat
