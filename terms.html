These rules apply to every order placed on the 3F Meatshop online shop. By placing an order you agree to them.

1. Ordering

  • Orders are placed on www.3fmeatshop.com with a customer account.
  • Every order is checked and confirmed by the shop before it is prepared — by call or text for cash on delivery, or after the payment is checked for GCash, Maya or bank payments.
  • The shop may decline an order (for example: the item is not available, the address is outside the delivery area, or the customer cannot be reached). The reason is shown on the order.
  • Bulk buyers with a supply agreement follow the terms of that agreement.

2. Prices and weight

  • Prices are in Philippine pesos (₱) and shown on the shop. Most products are sold per kilo; some are sold per pack, with the weight per pack indicated.
  • Meat is cut and weighed for each order, so the prepared weight can be slightly more or less than the weight ordered. The prepared weight is shown on the order before delivery.
  • The amount to pay is based on the weight delivered. For orders already paid online, any difference is settled at delivery — the difference is returned to the customer, or paid by the customer.

3. Payment

  • Accepted: cash on delivery, GCash / Maya, and bank transfer, as shown at checkout.
  • For GCash, Maya or bank transfer, the reference number or a photo of the receipt is required. The order is confirmed once the payment is checked.
  • For cash on delivery, please have the exact amount ready when the rider arrives.

4. Delivery

  • Delivery is within the barangays of Santa Ana, Cagayan.
  • Orders are delivered on the delivery date shown on the order — usually the day after the order is confirmed. There is no same-day delivery.
  • The delivery fee depends on the barangay and is shown at checkout. Orders above the free-delivery weight shown on the shop are delivered for free.
  • The rider calls or texts when the order is on the way. Please keep your phone on and have someone ready to receive the order. If no one can be reached, the order may be brought back to the shop and the delivery moved or cancelled.

5. Cancelling an order

  • The customer can cancel an order in My purchases any time until it is out for delivery. A reason is required.
  • Once the order is out for delivery it can no longer be cancelled online — call the shop.
  • The shop may cancel an order with a reason shown to the customer (for example: payment not received, customer not reachable, item not available).
  • When an order is cancelled, the items go back to the shop's stock.

6. Returns

⚠️ No returns once received. Please check your items when the rider arrives. Once the order is received and paid, items can no longer be returned.
  • If an item is wrong, damaged or not in good condition, refuse it at the door before paying. Refused items are taken back by the rider and are not charged.
  • Because meat is a perishable food, items cannot be returned after they are received — this protects food safety for every customer.

7. Refunds (orders paid online)

  • A full refund is given when a paid order is cancelled before it is out for delivery — by the customer or by the shop.
  • A partial refund is given for items refused at the door, items that could not be delivered, or a prepared weight lower than what was paid.
  • Refunds go back to the same GCash, Maya or bank account that was used to pay, within 3 business days after the shop confirms the refund. Bank processing may take longer.
  • Cash on delivery orders are only paid on delivery, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Delivery fees are refunded when the whole order is cancelled before delivery.

8. Vouchers

  • New customer voucher: ₱50 off the first order · retail customers only · minimum order ₱500 · valid 7 days from sign-up.
  • One voucher per order. Vouchers are applied at checkout, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • If the order that used the voucher is cancelled, the voucher can be used again until it expires.

9. Customer accounts

  • Keep your password private. Each customer uses their own account.
  • The shop may suspend an account used for fake orders or abuse.
  • How customer data is handled is explained in the Privacy Notice.

10. Changes

These rules may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version; the rules in force when an order is placed apply to that order.

11. Contact

3F Meatshop, Santa Ana, Cagayan, Philippines — call or message the shop, or message the shop's Facebook page. Questions about an order: open it in My purchases on www.3fmeatshop.com.
