Our story

3F Meatshop · Santa Ana, Cagayan

3F stands for what every order should be: Fresh, Frozen right, and Fine cuts.

3F Meatshop is a local meat shop in Santa Ana, Cagayan. It started with a simple idea — families here deserve meat they can trust, cut the way they need it, without the long trip to the market.

🐷 Whole pigs, cut herePork comes in as whole pigs and is butchered at the shop, so every cut is known and fresh.
🥟 Made in our own kitchenLongganisa, siomai, hamballs and samgyupsal are prepared in the shop's own kitchen.
⚖️ Weighed for every orderEach order is cut, weighed and packed for you, then checked again before it leaves.
🛵 To your doorDelivered within the barangays of Santa Ana, with the rider calling ahead.

Who we serve

Households, restaurants and companies — from a kilo for tonight's dinner to regular supply for a business.

Our promise

Honest weights, clean preparation and on-time delivery. If something is not right, tell the shop — Help › Contact the shop.
